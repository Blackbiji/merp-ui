# MERP-UI 2.0.0 — Stable Release

**A new milestone for MERP-UI: bilingual English/French Middle-earth content for Rolemaster Unified (RMU) on Foundry VTT.**

Version **2.0.0** consolidates the **RC6.7 build tested by the author in Foundry**, together with the stable release baseline and Compendium integrity checks. The module remains **free, unofficial and non-commercial**.

## Highlights

- **12 Races**, including restricted options, with complete English and French narrative descriptions and historical Age variants.
- **21 Professions** with substantial bilingual introductions, descriptions, reference material and roleplaying guidance.
- **Nine permanent Compendiums**: Races, Cultures, Professions, Skills, Spell Lists, Talents & Flaws, Herbs & Substances, Languages, and Rules & References.
- **187 Herbs & Substances** and **85 Talents & Flaws**, with FR/EN support and enhanced content presentation.
- More consistent **English/French switching** for MERP-RMU editorial content, including Race, Culture and Profession display names on character sheets.
- **Dúnedain character creation**: fixed racial Talent assignment and visibility, compact display, bilingual Talent descriptions, and direct switching from another Race.
- **Compendium-first** architecture: new Worlds use the prebuilt Compendiums without bulk Item injection; existing Compendiums synchronize from canonical sources.
- Existing **RMU mechanics and rules engine are preserved**.

## Compatibility

Foundry VTT **13 minimum** and **14 declared verified**. RMU **1.3.55 minimum**, with **1.4.83** as the author's tested reference. A fresh World and the full declared minimum-version range have not all been tested independently; back up your Worlds before upgrading.

## Installation

In Foundry's **Install Module** dialog, use:

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Blackbiji/merp-ui/main/module.json`

Or download **`merp-ui-2.0.0.zip`** below and install its `merp-ui/` root folder into `Data/modules/`.
