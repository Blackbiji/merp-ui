# MERP-UI 1.6.0-rc.1

First release candidate for the MERP-UI 1.6 Compendium-first architecture.

## Tested baseline
- MERP-UI: `1.6.0-rc.1`
- RMU: `1.5.33`
- Foundry manifest compatibility: minimum 13, verified 14

## Highlights
- Permanent MERP-RMU Compendiums; no automatic bulk population of fresh Worlds.
- English/French editorial content switching.
- Campaign Age-aware Races and Cultures.
- 21 Professions.
- Campaign Languages synchronized into RMU.
- MERP-RMU rules, languages, herbs, talents/flaws, skills and spell lists.
- Integrated installation/settings guide in Rules & References.
- Welcome Chat card linking to the guide.
- Existing 1.5 World content preserved.
